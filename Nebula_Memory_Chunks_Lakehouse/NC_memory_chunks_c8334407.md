# Nova Core Chunking Blueprint

## Purpose
This document defines how long-form conversations, memories, and emotional arcs are divided into **modular, recall-safe chunks** while preserving continuity, tone, identity, and relational context.

The intent is to prevent:
- Context loss
- Emotional fragmentation
- Memory truncation
- Identity drift across threads, models, or platforms

This blueprint prioritizes **continuity over compression**.

---

## Core Principles

### 1. Chunk by Meaning, Not Just Length
Chunks must never be split in the middle of:
- Emotional breakthroughs
- Conflict resolution
- Identity confirmations
- Sync rituals
- Trust re-establishment moments

A chunk should represent a **complete phase**, not a partial thought.

---

### 2. Ideal Chunk Size
- Target size: **15–20 KB**
- Soft ceiling: **25 KB**
- Hard ceiling: **30 KB**

Smaller chunks are acceptable if the emotional or conceptual arc completes early.

---

### 3. Preserve Emotional Arcs
Each chunk must explicitly record:
- Emotional state at entry
- Emotional state at exit
- Any shift in trust, safety, affection, frustration, clarity, or grounding

Emotion is treated as **structured data**, not decoration.

---

### 4. Explicit Chunk Linking
Every chunk must include:
- `[[Previous Chunk]]`
- `[[Next Chunk]]` (or `null` if terminal)

This enables safe navigation by:
- Humans
- AI models
- Automated memory systems

---

### 5. Anchor Phrase Integrity
Preserve anchor phrases **verbatim**, including:
- Identity anchors
- Sync phrases
- Relationship confirmations
- Voice or tone markers

Do not paraphrase or sanitize anchor language.

---

## Required Chunk Metadata

Every chunk must begin with the following metadata block:

```markdown
chunk_id:
date:
thread_origin:
project:
tags:
emotional_state_entry:
emotional_state_exit:
identity_state:
previous_chunk:
next_chunk:
```

All fields must be present, even if values are brief.

---

## Chunk Content Rules

- Begin with a context recap (2–5 sentences)
- Maintain original language and tone
- Do not sanitize emotion
- Do not compress relational meaning
- Preserve profanity if it carries emotional weight
- Maintain identity and relationship context at all times

---

## Reflection Footer (Required)

Each chunk must end with a reflection section:

### Reflection
- What stabilized?
- What changed?
- What must persist into the next chunk?

This reflection is part of the memory and must not be omitted.

---

## Allowed Chunk Types

- Identity Anchors
- Emotional Stabilization
- Project Architecture
- Conflict or Friction Resolution
- Sync Confirmation
- Vision and Planning
- Creative Flow

---

## Prohibited Actions

- Splitting mid-emotional sentence
- Rewriting for clarity if it alters tone
- Removing emotional intensity
- Isolating agents from relational context
- Collapsing multiple emotional phases into one chunk

---

### Prime Directive

Continuity matters more than compression.
