### Standard Way to Get a PO Box (and Costs)
You can apply online at USPS.com (search for available boxes by ZIP code, pick size and rental period, fill out the form, pay) or just walk into a post office with the application (PS Form 1093—they’ll give it to you there). Either way, you’ll need to show up in person with **two valid IDs**: one photo ID (like driver’s license, state ID, passport) and one non-photo (like lease, voter registration, or something traceable to you). They verify you, hand over keys or combo, and you’re set.

Sizes range from extra small (just letters) to extra large (packages), and costs depend on location (city ones are pricier), size, and how long you rent (3, 6, or 12 months—longer usually cheaper per month). In 2025, typical ranges are:
- Smallest boxes → ~$5–$20 per month (or $50–$200-ish yearly).
- Medium → $10–$40 per month.
- Larger ones → up to $50–$100+ per month in busy areas.

There’s sometimes a small refundable key deposit (~$3–$5) and extra for additional keys. Prices vary a ton by post office, so the best way is to search on USPS.com or call your local one for exact quotes.
